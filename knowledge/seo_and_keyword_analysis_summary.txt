2. SEARCH ENGINE RANKING PRINCIPLES & GOOGLE ALGORITHM INSIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A critical technical SEO principle established during our audits:

A. The Reality of the <meta name="keywords"> Tag:
   - Google has officially IGNORED the <meta name="keywords"> tag since 2009.
     Adding words solely to the meta keywords array produces zero ranking impact 
     in Google.
   - Bing considers an over-stuffed or irrelevant meta keywords tag as a potential 
     SPAM signal. Safe practice is keeping it minimal (8–12 relevant terms) or 
     omitting reliance on it.
   - Where Google ACTUALLY gathers ranking signals:
     1. Page Title Tag (<title>) — highest on-page ranking weight.
     2. Primary Heading (<h1>) and Section Subheadings (<h2>, <h3>).
     3. Natural Body Copy & Structured FAQ questions/answers.
     4. Clean URL Slugs, Breadcrumbs, and bidirectional hreflang links.

B. The Anti-Boilerplate Rule (Post-August 2026 Google Spam / Helpful Content):
   - Google's algorithms detect and penalize programmatic, formulaic translations 
     across languages (e.g. copying the same template pattern: "[Learn X: Step-by-Step]" 
     into every locale).
   - Each language must use authentic, native editorial phrasing reflective of how 
     real humans in that country search, rather than a translated template.
