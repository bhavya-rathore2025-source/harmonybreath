What is the Huberman psychological sigh?
AI Overview
The physiological sigh (often called the psychological sigh) is a fast breathing pattern consisting of two quick inhales and one long exhale, popularized by neuroscientist Dr. Andrew Huberman as the quickest way to reduce stress in real time.

Why does it use a double inhale?
