# HarmonyBreath — Master Project Vision & Roadmap

## OVERVIEW

HarmonyBreath is envisioned as the definitive, free, open, and privacy-first web platform for conscious breathwork. Rather than being limited to a single breathing method, HarmonyBreath serves as a universal hub uniting traditional pranayama, modern scientific breathwork protocols, and specialized performance breathing techniques into a single cohesive digital experience.

Every breathing technique on HarmonyBreath receives its own dedicated page featuring an interactive guided timer, tailored visual animations, acoustic cues, customizable parameters, safety guidelines, and evidence-based educational resources.

---

## CORE PHILOSOPHY & GUIDING PRINCIPLES

1. **Accessibility First**: 100% free forever. No paywalls, mandatory account creation, or hidden subscriptions.
2. **Privacy & Data Ownership**: All session tracking, personal records, and user preferences are stored strictly in local browser storage (localStorage). Zero tracking or telemetry.
3. **Scientific & Educational Grounding**: Clear explanation of physiological mechanisms (autonomic nervous system modulation, blood gas dynamics, vagus nerve stimulation, HRV optimization).
4. **Safety & Responsibility**: Transparent contraindications and safety protocols for every high-intensity or retention-based technique.
5. **Aesthetic & Sensory Excellence**: Calming, minimal dark-mode aesthetics, fluid micro-animations, customizable visual themes, and spatial audio cues.

---

## ARCHITECTURE: DEDICATED TECHNIQUE PAGES

Each breathing method is implemented on its own dedicated route (`/wim-hof`, `/box-breathing`, `/4-7-8`, `/coherent-breathing`, `/nadi-shodhana`, etc.) to provide focused environments optimized for each technique's unique timing, visual rhythm, and instructions.

### 1. Wim Hof Breathing (`/wim-hof`) — ACTIVE
* **Purpose**: Energy boost, stress resilience, immune system stimulation, cold adaptation.
* **Structure**: 30 cyclic deep breaths followed by a unforced exhalation hold (Phase 2) and a 15-second recovery hold (Phase 3).
* **Key Features**: 4 preset difficulty levels + custom hold target selector, interactive phase wheel, audio cues, local analytics.

### 2. Box Breathing / Tactical Breathing (`/box-breathing`) — PLANNED
* **Purpose**: Stress reduction, focus, high-pressure composure (popularized by Navy SEALs).
* **Structure**: 4 equal phases — Inhale (4s), Hold (4s), Exhale (4s), Hold (4s).
* **Key Features**: Adjustable square pace (3s to 8s), visual square expansion guide, calming audio metronome.

### 3. 4-7-8 Relaxing Breath (`/4-7-8`) — PLANNED
* **Purpose**: Deep relaxation, falling asleep, panic and anxiety neutralization.
* **Structure**: Inhale (4s), Full Hold (7s), Slow Exhale through mouth (8s).
* **Key Features**: Guided audio voice prompts, customizable round counts, sleep-mode night visualizer.

### 4. Coherent / Resonant Frequency Breathing (`/coherent-breathing`) — PLANNED
* **Purpose**: Maximizing Heart Rate Variability (HRV), emotional balance, cardiovascular resonance.
* **Structure**: Continuous smooth 5.5-second inhale and 5.5-second exhale (approx. 5.5 breaths/minute).
* **Key Features**: Variable frequency slider (4.5s to 6.5s per phase), ambient music synchronization, smooth sine-wave visualizer.

### 5. Nadi Shodhana / Alternate Nostril Breathing (`/nadi-shodhana`) — PLANNED
* **Purpose**: Hemispheric brain balance, mental clarity, energetic realignment.
* **Structure**: Alternating airflow between left and right nostrils with optional internal holds.
* **Key Features**: Step-by-step visual hand position guide, dual-channel airflow indicator.

### 6. Diaphragmatic / Belly Breathing (`/diaphragmatic-breathing`) — PLANNED
* **Purpose**: De-stressing, correcting shallow chest breathing habits, lower abdominal activation.
* **Structure**: Deep abdominal inhales with minimal chest movement.
* **Key Features**: Dual-ring abdominal expansion visualizer, posture & diaphragm mechanics guide.

### 7. Tummo Breathing (`/tummo`) — PLANNED
* **Purpose**: Inner heat generation, spiritual focus, deep meditative state (traditional Tibetan technique).
* **Structure**: Dynamic abdominal contractions combined with upper chest retains and visualization.
* **Key Features**: Advanced heat visualization graphics, structured safety guidelines.
